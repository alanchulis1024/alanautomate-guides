# Plan 7 dinners for $50 with ChatGPT

*Keyword: DINNER · Updated November 2026 · Needs: the free ChatGPT (its free plan includes unlimited text chats, chatgpt.com/pricing, checked November 21, 2026).*

One message, and ChatGPT plans a week of dinners on a budget, uses what you already have, and writes one shopping list. It's a plan, not magic: you still check the prices.

## The prompt (copy and paste)

```text
Plan 7 dinners for one week for [number] people, under $50 in total.
I already have: [list what's in your fridge and cupboard].
Reuse ingredients across the meals so nothing goes to waste.
Keep each dinner under 30 minutes. No [anything you don't eat].
Then give me ONE shopping list for everything I still need, grouped by section (produce, dairy, meat...),
with an estimated price for each item and the total.
```

## Make it better (follow-up messages)

- "Swap Wednesday for something with chicken."
- "Make it cheaper: under $40."
- "Turn the leftovers from Monday into Tuesday's lunch."

## The catch, plainly

- **It guesses prices.** ChatGPT doesn't see your store's real prices, so the total is an estimate.
- **Check the list in your store's app** (or its website) before you shop, and fix the plan if something costs more.
