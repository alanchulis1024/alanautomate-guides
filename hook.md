# Write a hook that shows the result first

*Keyword: HOOK · Updated November 2026 · Needs: nothing. A pen works.*

The first two seconds decide whether anyone watches the rest. My reels follow two rules for that moment, and my script checker refuses any video that breaks them.

## What you'll have at the end

A worksheet with 5 opening lines for your next post, one winner picked, and the post reordered so the result comes first.

## Rule 1: Show the ending first

Open on the finished thing: the final video, the before and after, the working result. Then say "here's how I built it" and go step by step.

People stay to find out *how*. They leave if they don't know *what* they're getting.

## Rule 2: Write 5 hooks, keep 1

Your first idea is rarely your best. Write five, each a different style, and pick one.

| # | Style | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Result | "This file runs my whole video page." |
| 2 | Contrast | "No editor. Python draws every frame." |
| 3 | Number | "1.5 seconds frozen? Rejected." |
| 4 | Question | "Mouth open, no voice?" |
| 5 | Mistake | "I almost posted as the wrong account." |

My limits:
- 8 words or fewer on screen.
- About 2 to 3 seconds when spoken.
- Shown on screen from the very first frame, so people watching on mute get it too.

## Rule 3: One video, one lesson

Every video teaches one thing the viewer can do. It ends with one small action they can take today.
