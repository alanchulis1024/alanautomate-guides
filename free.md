# Stop paying for apps that have free twins

*Keyword: FREE · Updated November 2026 · Prices checked on each app's own pricing page on November 21, 2026. Prices change, so check before you cancel anything.*

Four apps a lot of people pay for, and a free twin for each one. None of these are mine; I'm sharing them because they do the same job for $0 (each has a catch, listed plainly).

| You pay for | Price (checked Nov 21) | Free twin | The catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| ElevenLabs (AI voice) | Starter: $6 a month | **Kokoro** (Apache-2.0) | Fewer voices; the free online demo is the easy way, running it on your computer takes a few installs |
| Opus Clip (AI clips) | Starter: $15 a month | **OpenShorts** (MIT) | Free online plan: first video up to 60 min, then 20 min a month, with a watermark. No limit if you run it yourself (Docker) |
| Loom (screen recording) | Business: $18 per user a month | **Cap** (open source) | Free plan is for personal use: 25 share links a month, up to 5 minutes each |
| Notion (notes and docs) | Plus: $10 per member a month | **AppFlowy** (AGPL-3.0) | Fewer add-ons and templates than Notion; you download the desktop app |

That's **$49 a month** for the four paid plans above.

## Do this today

Open your bank app, find **one** of these subscriptions, and try its free twin for a day before you decide.
